GenshinERC – Elemental Reaction Craft
A Minecraft mod that fuses the elemental combat of Teyvat with the deep skill-based fighting system of EpicFight. Master elemental reactions wield unique weapon types and face challenging mobs all while experiencing fluid animation-driven combat.

Core Gameplay – Built on EpicFight
GenshinERC is an EpicFight-based expansion that adds a full elemental reaction system custom weapon movesets and a variety of new enemies. Every attack skill and reaction is seamlessly integrated into EpicFight’s animation and combo framework. You’ll still dodge parry and chain attacks like a true action game but now with the strategic depth of elemental interactions.

Elemental Reaction System
Apply elements to enemies and trigger powerful reactions
- Vaporize – Pyro + Hydro increases damage
- Melt – Pyro + Cryo increases damage
- Overloaded – Pyro + Electro explosive AoE
- Electro-Charged – Hydro + Electro continuous damage
- Frozen – Hydro + Cryo immobilizes enemies
- Swirl – Anemo + any element spreads elements
- Crystallize – Geo + any element creates shields
- Burning – Pyro + Dendro damage over time
  Each reaction has custom visual effects sounds and gameplay impact. Build your team swap characters and chain reactions to devastate your foes.

Weapons & Combat Styles
Weapons are more than stat sticks they define your fighting style
- Swords – Balanced swift combos
- Claymores – Heavy high-impact swings
- Polearms – Long reach sweeping attacks
- Bows – Ranged charged shots
- Catalysts – Elemental magic attacks
  Each weapon type has its own EpicFight moveset complete with light attacks heavy attacks and weapon skills. Infuse your weapon with elements to trigger reactions on hit.

Mobs & Bosses
Face a growing roster of elemental enemies
- Hilichurls – Basic melee and ranged variants
- Abyss Mages – Shielded elemental casters
- Ruin Guards – Heavy mechanical foes with weak points
- Bosses – Unique multi-phase encounters with elemental mechanics
  Enemies use EpicFight AI meaning they dodge block and counter not just walk toward you. Bosses require mastering reactions and weapon matchups to defeat.

Designed for Expansion
GenshinERC is built to be modular. The core combat is powered by EpicFight and all custom content is designed to be easily extended or replaced.
> Requires EpicFight and its dependencies to run
